# Tomato (টমেটো)

Source: FarmMarket agronomy notes, general guidance — verify with local extension officer.

- Water tomato plants at the base, not on the leaves, to reduce disease.
- Support plants with stakes or string so fruit does not touch the soil.
- Remove yellow or spotted leaves early and keep them away from healthy plants.
- Harvest tomatoes when they are fully coloured but still firm.
- Handle harvested tomatoes gently; bruised fruit spoils quickly.
- Store tomatoes in a cool, shaded, ventilated place, not in direct sun.
- Sell ripe tomatoes soon after harvest; do not store soft or damaged fruit with good fruit.
- Ask your local extension officer before using any pesticide or fertiliser.
