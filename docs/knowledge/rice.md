# Rice (ধান)

Source: FarmMarket agronomy notes, general guidance — verify with local extension officer.

- Keep the field water level even during the growing season; avoid letting young plants dry out or stay flooded for long.
- Watch for yellowing leaves and empty grains, which can be signs of nutrient stress or disease.
- Harvest when most grains are firm and golden, and avoid harvesting in heavy rain.
- Dry harvested paddy in thin layers in the sun, turning it often, before storage.
- Store dried paddy in a cool, dry place in clean sacks or bins, raised off the ground.
- Check stored grain regularly for moisture, insects and bad smell.
- Ask your local extension officer before using any pesticide or fertiliser.
