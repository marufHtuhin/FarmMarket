# Weather basics for harvest timing

Source: FarmMarket agronomy notes, general guidance — verify with local extension officer.

- Heavy rain near harvest can damage ripe crops and make fields hard to work in.
- Harvesting before a long wet spell can reduce losses, if the crop is ready.
- Very hot weather makes soft fruit spoil faster after picking.
- Keep harvested produce dry and covered during transport in rain.
- Wet produce stored in closed sacks can rot quickly; dry it first where possible.
- Check the forecast before planning a harvest day, and be ready to change the plan.
- If the weather is uncertain, ask your local extension officer for advice.
