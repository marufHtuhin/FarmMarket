# Banana (কলা)

Source: FarmMarket agronomy notes, general guidance — verify with local extension officer.

- Plant bananas in a sheltered spot; strong wind can snap the stems.
- Support heavy bunches with a prop so the plant does not fall over.
- Keep the soil moist but not waterlogged, and mulch around the base.
- Harvest a bunch when the fingers are full and rounded, before they turn fully yellow on the plant.
- Cut the bunch with a clean, sharp tool and carry it without dropping or squeezing.
- Store bananas in a cool, shaded place; keep them out of direct sun.
- Sell ripe bananas quickly, as they soften fast once yellow.
- Ask your local extension officer before using any pesticide or fertiliser.
