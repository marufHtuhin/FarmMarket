# FAOSTAT notes

Source: FarmMarket agronomy notes, general guidance — verify with local extension officer.

- FAOSTAT is the Food and Agriculture Organization's statistics database.
- Its producer prices are national figures, usually reported per year or per month.
- FAOSTAT prices are not daily market prices for a local bazaar.
- A national average can hide large differences between districts and seasons.
- Use FAOSTAT to compare crops and countries over time, not to set today's selling price.
- For a selling decision, compare prices at nearby markets first.
