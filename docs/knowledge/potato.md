# Potato (আলু)

Source: FarmMarket agronomy notes, general guidance — verify with local extension officer.

- Plant potatoes in loose, well-drained soil and avoid waterlogged ground.
- Mound soil around the stems as plants grow so tubers stay covered and green-free.
- Stop watering shortly before harvest so the skins firm up.
- Harvest carefully with a fork or by hand to avoid cutting the tubers.
- Let harvested potatoes dry in the shade for a short time before storage.
- Store potatoes in a cool, dark, ventilated place; keep them out of sunlight.
- Check stored potatoes regularly and remove any soft, sprouting or rotten ones.
- Ask your local extension officer before using any pesticide or fertiliser.
