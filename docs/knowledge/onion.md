# Onion (পেঁয়াজ)

Source: FarmMarket agronomy notes, general guidance — verify with local extension officer.

- Grow onions in well-drained soil; standing water rots the bulbs.
- Keep the field weeded, as onions compete poorly with weeds.
- Harvest onions when the leaves fall over and the necks feel soft and dry.
- Cure harvested onions in a dry, shaded, airy place before storage.
- Store cured onions in a cool, dry, ventilated place, spread out or in nets.
- Do not store damp or bruised onions with good ones; they spoil the rest.
- Check stored onions regularly and remove any soft or sprouting bulbs.
- Ask your local extension officer before using any pesticide or fertiliser.
